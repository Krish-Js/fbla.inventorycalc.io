Quick planning notes

Important assumptions to verify with your team.

  • Your table's pack costs add to $316.82, not $315.95.
  • At the listed pack quantities and sell prices, maximum sales are $751.00, with estimated gross profit of $434.18.
  • $30 students × 10 points × $1/point gives $300 revenue; at $0.45 cost per redeemed point, gross profit is $165.
  • $700 revenue is not the result of 30 students each buying 10 points at $1 per point; that scenario yields $300. Check which assumption you intended.
